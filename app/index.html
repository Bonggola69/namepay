Send

Pay someone by their name, for example hanta or hanta.ai. The name is resolved to an address on chain before you confirm.

Token payments need two wallet confirmations: first an approval for the exact amount, then the payment itself. No unlimited approvals are used.

Payment requests

Ask someone to pay you. Create a request to their name, and they pay it from this page. Requests expire at the time you set.

Pay a request

Split payment

Pay up to 20 people in one transaction. Each row is one name and one amount.

Recurring payments

Fund a schedule up front and it pays out one period at a time. No allowance is requested: the money is held in the contract as escrow, and you can cancel any time to get the unspent part back.

My subscriptions

Tip link

Make a public tip page for any name and share the link. Anyone who opens it can tip that name.

Recent payments read from the contract's on-chain events. Names are resolved for display.
